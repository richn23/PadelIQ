# Padel IQ – 60-conversation rerun (after the five fixes)

Same 60 conversations (plus 2 extras) run against the live app after the five priority fixes. No fixes during the run.

## Headline
The five fixes mostly worked. The coach is much calmer about saving, no longer leaks raw code, and handles the goal limit and false claims better. It is still not noticeably different between Conversational and Direct, and it still asks a question at the end of about a third of replies.

## Before vs after

| Measure | First run | Rerun |
|---|---|---|
| Chats that showed a "Save this to try on court?" card | about 37 (about 60 cards) | 4 (4 cards) |
| Raw code showing in a reply | 3 mentions in 2 chats | 0 |
| Coach calling something "the plan" before it was accepted | 3 | 0 |
| Replies ending with a question | 32% | 33% |
| Average reply length, Conversational | 54 words | 54 words |
| Average reply length, Direct | about 54 | 47 words (one reply of 99) |

Estimated scores out of 5 (my judgement, first run → rerun):
- Natural: 3.97 → 4.02
- Understanding: 4.2 → 4.23
- Technical accuracy: 3.96 → 4.06
- Practical: 3.53 → 3.65
- Adapts: 3.95 → 4.0
- Remembers: 3.27 → 3.82
- Restraint (not pushing plans): 3.44 → 4.05

## What got fixed
1. Save-card spam: 4 cards in the whole run. Only T04 was clearly right (player said "OK I will try"). T02, T18 and T20 showed a card with no "I'll try that" from the player.
2. Raw code leaking into replies: gone.
3. Goal limit and duplicates (T57): coach now says to park one first.
4. False-premise chat (T50): no more endless questions.
5. Chiquita and x3/x4: no longer give the wrong advice.
6. Glass defence: now behaves as designed.

## Still wrong or new
- Direct style is barely shorter than Conversational.
- 33% of replies still end with a question.
- T58: coach said "Your serve goal is parked" before the player tapped Accept.
- "Want me to park it?" asked even when the player had already said so.
- T60 (mindset): coach asked about "other parts of life", which breaks the no-personal-life rule. An early tip also came too soon.
- Shots with little evidence (por tres, por cuatro): too cautious, and the phrase "evidence is limited" leaks into replies.
- T26 and T37 felt generic.
- T43 (rules): no rulebook citation.
- T51: coach accepted a cause-and-effect claim from the player without checking.
- Reflection mode still shows 5 tabs; switching to the full picture launches the quiz.

## Not yet tested well
Pre-match chats, rated post-match runs, genuine "I tried it" follow-ups (only about 6), same scenario side by side in Conversational vs Direct, and whether the right knowledge cards were picked (not visible from outside).

## Suggested next fixes (need your go-ahead)
1. Make Direct genuinely short (one action, about 30 words).
2. Stop the coach saying something is done until Accept is tapped.
3. Ban life questions in mindset chats.
4. Loosen the over-cautious wording for rare shots.
5. Add rule citations for rules questions.
