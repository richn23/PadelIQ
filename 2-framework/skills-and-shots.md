# Padel IQ: Skills and shots (draft v0.1)

Structure: **5 areas** (the pentagon) contain **skills**. Most skills are **shots**, grouped as attacking, defensive, transition and specialist. Every item has a glossary entry.

- **Beginner** = new to the game: getting the serve in, getting the ball back, knowing where to stand.
- **Pro** = coming soon (not assessed).
- ✓ = checked in the app. Others are glossary only.
- Everything marked draft. Names and levels to check with coaches.

## Attacking shots
| | Skill / shot | Also called | Area | From level | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ✓ | Serve | Saque | Attack | Beginner | Underarm serve: bounce the ball behind the line and hit it at or below waist height, diagonally into the box. |
| ✓ | Volley | Volea | Attack | D | Hitting the ball before it bounces, usually at the net, forehand or backhand. |
|  | Block volley |  | Attack | C | A short, firm volley with almost no swing that uses the ball's own pace. |
|  | Drop volley | Dejada de volea | Attack | B | A soft volley that drops just over the net. |
| ✓ | Basic overhead |  | Attack | D | Hitting a high ball above your head with control rather than power. |
| ✓ | Bandeja |  | Attack | C | A sliced overhead hit at about head height, flatter and slower than a smash. It keeps you at the net. |
| ✓ | Víbora |  | Attack | B | A faster, more aggressive bandeja with side spin, aimed low into the side glass. |
| ✓ | Smash | Remate | Attack | C | A full, hard overhead hit downwards. |
|  | Gancho | Hook | Attack | B | A quick overhead hit in front of the body from the side, near the net, without turning fully sideways. |
|  | Rulo | Topspin smash | Attack | A | A smash with heavy topspin that kicks up off the back glass. |
| ✓ | Smash por tres | x3 | Attack | A | A smash hit so hard it bounces out over the side wall. |
|  | Smash por cuatro | x4 | Attack | A | A smash that bounces back over the net, or out through the side door, on your side. |
| ✓ | Bajada |  | Attack | B | An attacking shot hit after the ball has come off the back glass and risen high. |
|  | Drop shot | Dejada | Attack | B | A soft shot that lands just over the net and dies. |
