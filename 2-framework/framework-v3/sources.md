# Sources and evidence boundaries

Checked 26 October 2026. This file was created for the downloadable player-framework package. It is not a copy of a separately mentioned repository sources file, which was not available in this workspace.

## Rules

[FIP Rules of Padel, in force from 1 January 2026](https://www.padelfip.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/FIP-Rules-of-Padel.pdf): authority for service/return legality, point-ending exits and authorized outside play. The older date in the URL is not the effective date in the document. Use the rule text when simplified summaries differ. Rules do not establish the framework's Beginner–A descriptors or assessment cutoffs.

## Local level conventions

[Viya Padel level structure, Dubai Golf](https://im.dubaigolf.com/media/g5nhaykq/viya-padel-level-structure.pdf): useful local benchmark spanning technique, tactics, positioning and movement. Its B is 5, B+ is 5.5 and A is 6. PadelIQ's existing letters are not established equivalents. A numerical mapping needs coach calibration; no crosswalk is published in this revision.

## Teaching coverage

[LTA basic-shot tutorials](https://www.lta.org.uk/advantage-home/content/padel-tutorials-how-to-play-basic-shots/): supports keeping familiar groundstroke, volley, glass and bandeja teaching vocabulary. It does not validate detailed self-assessment statements or an overall rating formula.

## Content validation

[Escudero-Tena and colleagues, Technical-tactical objectives by stages for padel (TTOSP), 2025](https://ccd.ucam.edu/index.php/revista/en/article/view/2257): an existing instrument for technical–tactical objectives by stage. Twelve experts rated the initial 57 items for appropriateness and wording. The authors used Aiken's V with confidence intervals and revised poorly rated items. This is relevant prior work, so the categorical claim “no validated padel framework exists” should not be used.

A narrower defensible statement is: “We have not identified a validated adult recreational competency-to-rating framework matching PadelIQ's intended use.” This is a bounded research conclusion, not proof that none exists.

Expert content ratings can support relevance and clarity. They do not independently establish observer agreement on actual players, stable score boundaries, or predictive validity. PadelIQ descriptors remain drafts until reviewed and tested. The published instrument's wording is not reproduced in this package.

## Why isolated accuracy is insufficient

[Mendes and colleagues, Technical performance and aerobic fitness in Padel amateurs players with different practice levels, Retos, 2022](https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/8558624.pdf): in a convenience sample of 49 adult male players, the closed accuracy task did not significantly differentiate the two groups. This is a reason to investigate decisions and match context alongside execution. It does not prove that PadelIQ's tactical anchors distinguish levels, or that accuracy never differentiates players.

## What remains a PadelIQ design choice

The five areas, checklist phrasing, letter anchors, placement of specialist introductory rows and Mentality behaviors are PadelIQ editorial/product constructs. They are not established by federation rules. Pro-match shot frequencies can inform coverage, but cannot establish recreational level thresholds or mandatory shot requirements.

The player CSV deliberately contains no mandatory attempt counts, marked-target tests or video requirements. The separate coach protocol contains provisional methods for later testing, not conditions of app use.
