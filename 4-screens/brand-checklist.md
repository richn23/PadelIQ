# Padel IQ brand checklist

Based on brand-sheet-v1.png (assets folder). Tick items off as you fix them.

## Must fix
- [ ] Typo on mascot sheet: "CELERATING" should be "CELEBRATING"
- [ ] "B" level badge: white letter on lime is hard to read. Use a dark letter or a different colour
- [ ] Matches tab icon: trophy suggests winning and rankings. Use a racket, ball or calendar instead

## Decide
- [ ] Name styling: "Padel IQ" or "Padel iQ" (lowercase i)? Pick one and use it everywhere, including the app store name
- [ ] Level badge colours: red for Pro can look like an error. Consider one colour that gets stronger with each level. Letters must stay readable (colour-blind players)
- [ ] Axis icons: five colours clash with level colours. Consider one colour (white chalk + lime accent) so it works on all three courts
- [ ] App icon: choose one of the three (blue textured is the strongest)
- [ ] Mascot: two looks exist (blue lines on white, white lines on blue). Fine as light and dark versions, but make it deliberate
- [ ] Lob pose (white-on-white sheet): check the figure has a racket

## Files still needed
- [ ] Logo: light, dark and one-colour versions as separate files, transparent background (SVG or PNG)
- [ ] Mascot: 7 poses as separate files, transparent background, no labels
- [ ] Mascot poses in white chalk (for the three court colours)
- [ ] Court backgrounds: blue, clay, green (seamless tiles)
- [ ] Chalk line pieces: straight, corner, curve, box, circle, arrow
- [ ] Icons: 5 axes, 6 levels, 5 tabs, as separate files
- [ ] App icon at the sizes the phone stores ask for (can be produced from the chosen design)

## Test before making everything
- [ ] Put text on a panel over the blue court texture on a real phone screen. Is it readable?
- [ ] Check pentagon and progress bar colours on blue, clay and green
- [ ] Check axis icons at actual phone size (small)
- [ ] Build a few screens on blue first. Only make clay and green once the layout works
